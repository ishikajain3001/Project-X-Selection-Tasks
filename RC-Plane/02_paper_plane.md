# Problem 2 — The Paper Plane Challenge
**Marks:** 25 (+5 bonus)

Build a paper airplane that stays airborne for the **longest duration**, rather than merely covering the greatest distance.

## Constraints
- One standard A4 sheet; folding and cutting allowed.
- No tape, glue, paper clips, or external propulsion.
- Manual launch only; test indoors or in approximately wind-free conditions.

## Part A — Design
1. Construct the aircraft and document steps with photos or diagrams.
2. Explain your choice of wing shape, dimensions, and configuration.

## Part B — Aerodynamics
1. How does the airplane generate lift?
2. Discuss lift, weight, drag, and thrust during launch and glide (including the absence of sustained propulsion).
3. Explain wing area, wing loading, centre of gravity, and stability.
4. Why might this design stay airborne longer than a conventional dart? Distinguish minimum sink rate from maximum glide distance.

## Part C — Experiment
1. Complete at least five flight trials of your final design under comparable conditions.
2. Record each flight duration and compute the mean.
3. Suggest at least two modifications and explain how you would test them while controlling other variables.

**Bonus (+5):** Build a second design and compare average flight durations using repeatable trials.

**Deliverable:** Construction photos, aerodynamic explanation, trial table, results, and short video of at least one flight.

### Submission expectations
State assumptions, cite sources, and justify choices using engineering reasoning. Diagrams, calculations, and comparison tables are encouraged. AI tools may support research, but you must be able to defend your work.
