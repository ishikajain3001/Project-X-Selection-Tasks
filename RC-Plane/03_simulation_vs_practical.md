# Problem 3 — Simulation vs Practical Development
**Marks:** 25 (+5 bonus)

Your team must build an RC aircraft for a national aeromodelling competition with limited time, money, and resources. Some teammates advocate extensive simulation; others favor rapid physical prototyping. As lead designer, choose and defend a development approach.

## Tasks
1. Explain advantages and limitations of simulation-led design and practical prototype-led design.
2. Compare cost, time, accuracy, iteration speed, failure detection, and final performance.
3. Propose a workflow from mission requirements and calculations through design, manufacturing, testing, and refinement.
4. Name at least three parameters to study through simulation and three to verify physically.
5. Explain how to validate simulations using measurements.
6. Your simulated plane performs well, but the prototype cannot take off: give plausible causes and a systematic diagnostic plan.
7. You may discuss XFLR5, OpenVSP, Fusion 360, SolidWorks, ANSYS, etc.; running software is not mandatory.

**Bonus (+5):** Plan a realistic four-week schedule with contingency time.

**Deliverable:** 2–3 page technical proposal, comparison table, and development flowchart.

### Submission expectations
State assumptions, cite sources, and justify choices using engineering reasoning. Diagrams, calculations, and comparison tables are encouraged. AI tools may support research, but you must be able to defend your work.
