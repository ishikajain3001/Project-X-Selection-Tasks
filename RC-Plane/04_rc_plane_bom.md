# Problem 4 — Bill of Materials for an RC Aircraft
**Marks:** 25 (+5 bonus)

Prepare a complete bill of materials (BOM) for a functional, electric, single-motor fixed-wing RC aircraft. Do not purchase parts for this assignment.

## Aircraft brief
| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Wingspan | 1–1.2 m |
| Configuration | Conventional or high wing |
| Propulsion | Single electric motor |
| Control | Wireless remote control |
| Structure | Lightweight and repairable |
| Takeoff | Ground or hand launch |

## Part A — Identify components
Include airframe and reinforcement, motor, ESC, propeller, battery, transmitter, receiver, servos, landing gear if needed, connectors, wiring, adhesives, and other assembly materials.

## Part B — Compare options
For **every major component**, recommend at least two alternatives. Record specifications, quantity, approximate INR price, and source/purchase link. Use or extend this template:

|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|

## Part C — Justify selections
1. Explain the motor–propeller match and how you would check available thrust.
2. Determine a suitable ESC rating and electrical margin.
3. Explain battery selection and trade-offs.
4. State servo count and intended control surfaces.
5. Select structural material considering strength, mass, cost, and repairability.
6. Explain electrical and mechanical compatibility checks, including transmitter–receiver compatibility.

## Part D — Budget
1. Estimate total build cost.
2. Identify safe cost-saving opportunities.
3. Produce budget-oriented and performance-oriented configurations.
4. Choose one for the competition and justify it.

**Bonus (+5):** Estimate total takeoff mass and assess whether propulsion is sufficient using stated assumptions.

**Deliverable:** Complete BOM spreadsheet or table, source links, totals, and technical rationale.

### Submission expectations
State assumptions, cite sources, and justify choices using engineering reasoning. Diagrams, calculations, and comparison tables are encouraged. AI tools may support research, but you must be able to defend your work.
